About MedEd Tools

A collaborative project to support the medical education community

Purpose

MedEd Tools offers practical resources designed to support medical educators in their teaching, assessment, and research activities. These tools aim to make evidence-based approaches more accessible to busy faculty members and researchers.

Who?

Hi! I'm Matt Bowker. I'm a senior lecturer in Medical Education at University of Sunderland. I'm a medical doctor by background and have particular interests in teaching communication skills, simulation, and gender-based violence.

Collaborative Approach

This project is developed by and for medical educators. We welcome contributions, feedback, and collaboration from anyone in the medical education community to help improve these resources.
